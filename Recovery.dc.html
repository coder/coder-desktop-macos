Rules for the strip
1
One row, always. Show the highest-severity live condition — blocked > queued > stale context > retrying — and count the rest; the disclosure expands to the full list.
2
Every row carries its action inline (Refresh, Retry, Learn more) or it doesn’t belong in the strip. A notice with no next step is a log line, not UI.
3
Transient conditions (retry backoff) auto-clear; sticky ones (capacity, stale context) persist until resolved. Never animate the strip’s height while the transcript is streaming.
4
Native: mirror a blocked or queued state onto the menu-bar item and the Dock badge, so someone who walked away learns their agent is stuck without checking the window.
