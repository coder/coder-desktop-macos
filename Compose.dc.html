Disabled, named, and honest: the pill states the organization it looked in. Its tooltip carries the next step (“An admin configures chat models per organization”), and a loading list shows a spinner in the same slot instead of nothing.
Native affordances this composer should have
↑↓ recalls your last prompts
On an empty composer, walk this chat’s prompt history the way a shell does. The SDK call already exists with no caller.
Drop files onto the window
Paste is handled; drag is not. A Mac user drags a screenshot from the desktop onto the composer — accept the drop, show the insertion target, upload on release.
A real Chat menu
The window has no menu commands, so every shortcut is invisible and undiscoverable. A Chat menu (New, Search, Stop, Compact, Clear, Archive) puts them where macOS teaches people to look — and gives Help’s search something to find.
Services & Share
“Send to Coder Agent” as a system Service means any selected text in any app becomes a prompt. A share item on a chat hands its link to Messages or Mail without a copy‑paste round trip.
